Every finding says how sure it is.

The rules are enforced in code, not left to the model’s mood. A forum post can’t be promoted to a fact, and old sources get flagged.

confirmed

Backed by an official source, such as the company, a university or an embassy.

candidate-reported

Comes from forums, reviews or interview videos. Useful, but never marked confirmed, and ReadyWise shows how many separate sources agree.

inferred

A reasonable expectation from your role or the job description, with nothing external behind it yet.

Scores come from rubrics and repeated observations. They describe practice performance, not your chance of being hired.
