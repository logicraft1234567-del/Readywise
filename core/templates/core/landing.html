Say something wrong and the panel knows.

Public facts in your answers, such as prices, dates and figures, are checked online while you talk. If one is wrong, the panel gets the verified figure and the source, then asks you about it. A check that is slow never stops the interview.

Formats set for you

The format moves from standard to challenging, high pressure, executive or unknown questions as your scores rise. It stays professional.

Practise as a team

Invite up to four teammates by email. You share one workspace, split the plan and agree one time for the practice interview.

Twelve languages

Interview in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Arabic, Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Hindi or Mandarin. Spoken voices are English only. Other languages use your browser’s voice.

Pitches and hackathons

For products, ReadyWise finds the competitors that really compete, checks each one, and drops names that only share a keyword.

Practice, not prediction.

Scores describe how you performed in practice. They never say whether you will be hired, admitted or given a visa. Research that can’t be reached, such as login-walled sites, is left out, not guessed.

confirmed

Backed by an official source, such as the company, a university or an embassy.

candidate-reported

From forums, reviews or videos. Useful, never marked confirmed, and ReadyWise shows how many sources agree.

inferred

A reasonable expectation from your role or the job description, with nothing external behind it yet.
